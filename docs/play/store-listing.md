## Full description

Bukin Mic Recorder is a straightforward audio recorder for compatible USB microphones and your phone's built-in microphone.

RECORD
- Save uncompressed WAV audio with a live waveform and timer.
- Automatically select a supported recording format and see the saved audio format.
- Continue recording with the screen off or while using another app.
- Split long recordings into 30-, 45- or 60-minute segments.
- Save in app storage or a folder you choose.

CHECK YOUR MICROPHONE
Use Mic Test to see the live input level without saving a recording.

LISTEN AND ORGANIZE
Play recordings, adjust playback speed and view file details. Delete recordings individually or select several at once.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, analytics, account or internet permission. The app does not upload recordings. If you choose a folder managed by a cloud provider, that provider may sync the files under its own settings and policy.

GOOD TO KNOW
This is a microphone recorder, not a phone-call recorder. Microphone compatibility and recording formats depend on the microphone, phone and Android version. Calls or other apps may interrupt microphone access.

Uncompressed WAV files can be large. Recordings kept in app storage are removed when you uninstall the app; export anything you want to keep first.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer.
