## Prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a polished Google Play feature graphic for the existing Android app Bukin Mic Recorder. Landscape 1024 by 500 pixels (or exact same 2.048:1 ratio), opaque image, no transparency. Restrained contemporary design with existing brand blue #2D65A0 and white, subtle lighter blue accents. Abstract audio waveform as the main visual motif, generous negative space, clean confident sans-serif typography. Exact text only: 'Bukin Mic Recorder' and smaller 'Uncompressed WAV recording'. All key text and waveform centered safely away from outer edges. Keep it simple and readable at small size. No giant microphone logo duplicating the app icon. No screenshots, no invented app interface, no device frames, no store badges, no awards, no claims of best quality, no decorative mockup scene, no additional words. Finished flat store banner, not an image of a banner.
