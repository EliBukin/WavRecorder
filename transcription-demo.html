Bukin Mic Recorder

Transcription foreground service demonstration

This 92-second demonstration of Bukin Mic Recorder 1.5 shows a user transcribing a recording in Hebrew. The user opens the Transcripts tab, taps Transcribe a recording, picks a recording, chooses Hebrew and taps Transcribe. The user then leaves the app: the transcription keeps running on the phone, with a progress notification that has a Cancel action. Back in the app, the finished transcript opens from its card. The video has no audio; dots show the taps.
